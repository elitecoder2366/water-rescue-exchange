## Research gap

Water allocation, water trading, canal scheduling, sensor automation, and quantum optimization each have prior work. This prototype does not claim to be the first to combine them. The candidate direction to investigate is a tested workflow that connects verified surplus, recipient deadlines, route feasibility, and independently validated transfer recommendations, then compares optimizers on reproducible cases.
